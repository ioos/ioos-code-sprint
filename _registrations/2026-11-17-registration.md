## Travel

The Gulf of America Coastal Ocean Observing System (GCOOS) will be hosting the Code Sprint and has some limited funding to provide travel vouchers for those in-person IOOS Regional Association partners in need. Please indicate your need for travel support in the registration form above, and one of the GCOOS organizers will reach out to you.  If travel support is approved, the travel voucher will be paid with check in advance of the event.
